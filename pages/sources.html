Sources & Citations

Every statistic on this site is sourced. We show our work.

We don’t ask you to take our word for it.

Every major statistic and claim on SVR-United.com is sourced from primary government data, peer-reviewed academic research, nonpartisan policy organizations, or official congressional vote records. We cite the original source — not a news article about the source.

If you believe anything on this site is inaccurate or outdated, we want to know. A movement built on facts has nothing to fear from scrutiny. SVR-United.com is committed to accuracy above everything else.

A note on our sourcing standard: Every statistic on SVR-United.com is sourced from primary government data (CDC, BLS, CBO, USDA, Treasury, FEC), peer-reviewed academic research, nonpartisan policy organizations, or official congressional vote records. We cite original sources, not secondary reporting.

Vote counts are sourced directly from official House and Senate voting records at clerk.house.gov and senate.gov. Economic data comes from BLS, BEA, CBO, and the Federal Reserve. Legal citations link directly to Supreme Court and congressional records.

SVR-United.com welcomes corrections. If you believe any statistic is inaccurate or outdated, contact us. A movement built on facts has nothing to fear from scrutiny.
