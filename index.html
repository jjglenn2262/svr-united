A movement. A demand. A voice.
We are not a party. We are not a candidate.
We are millions of ordinary Americans
who are done waiting.

We are done waiting for politicians to do what we elected them to do. Done waiting for change that never comes. Done watching the same problems get worse while the people we put in power collect checks from the people causing those problems.

We have three demands. They are not radical. They are not partisan. They are what the overwhelming majority of Americans — Republican, Democrat, and Independent — say they want when someone actually asks them.

Our three demands
1
Get money out of politics.
Billionaires and corporations have bought our government. Dark money floods our elections. The people we elect answer to donors, not voters. Full disclosure. Public campaign financing. Citizens United overturned.
2
Term limits for Congress. Reform the Supreme Court.
Career politicians who never face real accountability are not democracy. Lifetime appointments to the most powerful court in the land are not democracy. Power must return to the people, not the permanent political class.
3
Real gun laws. Now.
Not a ban. Not a confiscation. Universal background checks. Red flag laws. Age limits on military-style weapons. Ninety percent of Americans — in every state, every party — support these measures. They have been blocked for decades. No more.

These are not complicated. They are not controversial. They have majority support in every poll, in every state, in every party.

And they have been blocked. Every single time. For decades.

We are done asking nicely.

We are joining together in one loud, unified voice that cannot be ignored. When we vote together — across issues, across states, across party lines — we become a force that changes what is possible.

We are not asking you to become a Democrat. We are asking you to look honestly at who has been blocking the things you say you want — and vote accordingly.

Before we tell you what SVR stands for — read what we’ve put together. Look at the issues. Look at the votes. Look at the record. Judge for yourself.
